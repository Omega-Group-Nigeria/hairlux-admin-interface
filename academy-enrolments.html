    Enrolments

    Ads point to the Academy landing page programmes.html, which leads to the enrolment form (enrol-now.html). The visitor picks an in-branch training and class date, or an online course, and pays through Paystack: the full fee, or for in-branch training a deposit that holds the seat. The balance is due before the class starts: paid online from the link in their email, or at the branch (record it here or under Cohorts). A learner who still owes cannot be marked present.
