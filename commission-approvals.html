    Commission Plans

    Bookings completed under a Commission Plan that requires approval record their commission as Pending. Payroll only pays Approved commission.

    What happens when you approve, by the state of the payroll period:
    • Awaiting Release: the staff member's payslip is recalculated and their locked wallet credit is adjusted automatically.
    • Draft, never generated: generate the period after approving.
    • Draft that already credited wallets (sent back for correction): locked here. Generate the Draft period first.
    • Released: locked here. Use a Bonus payroll adjustment instead.
