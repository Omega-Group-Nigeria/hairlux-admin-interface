    Recalculate commissions

    Finds bookings in the date range whose commission was recorded at 0% (no active plan at the time) and recalculates them with the staff member's current Commission Plan (rate and eligible services, including services added after completion).
    How each payroll period is handled: Awaiting Release (generated, not yet approved): records are corrected and each affected staff member's payslip and locked wallet credit are recalculated automatically on Apply. Draft, never generated: records are corrected; generate the period afterwards as usual. Draft that already credited wallets (sent back for correction): not changed; click Generate for that period first, then run this again. Released: never changed; listed with the shortfall so you can add a Bonus adjustment.
