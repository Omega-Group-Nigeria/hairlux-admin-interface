    Company Documents

    Each document is signed by the staff it is addressed to (everyone, chosen roles, chosen branches or chosen staff). Staff sign by typing their full name. Saving a new version makes everyone it is addressed to sign again. Older signatures are never erased: they stay on record as a true account of what each person agreed to at the time. Changing only who must sign keeps the signatures already given.
