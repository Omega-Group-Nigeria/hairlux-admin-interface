    Ad Bookings

    Ads point to the landing page salon.html, which leads to the booking form (book-now.html). The visitor picks a branch, services and time and pays a deposit (or the full price) through Monnify, and the booking is logged with its source, e.g. Facebook Ad. A customer account is created for new emails. At the branch, staff verify the reservation code and collect the balance.
