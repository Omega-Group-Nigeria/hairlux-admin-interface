    Cancel vendor order

    The customer is refunded what they paid for these items, to the wallet. The vendor is emailed your reason.

    Record a return

    The customer is refunded the price of what came back, to the wallet (the delivery fee is not refunded). The vendor's share comes off their balance; if it was already paid, it comes off their next payout. The vendor is emailed.
